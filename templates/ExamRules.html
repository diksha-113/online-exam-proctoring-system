Rules & Test Environment

Monitors your camera and microphone during the exam. Grant permissions when prompted.

If the camera doesn't detect your face, it is marked as a violation.

Ensure the camera can clearly focus on your face during the exam.

Once your face is detected, the system captures the first photo.

Only you should be in front of the camera. Keep background clean.

Take the exam in a quiet environment. Any detected sound is recorded.

Test Environment Setup

  • Sit at a clean desk or table.
  • Take the exam in the same room scanned during proctoring setup.
  • The room must be quiet. No music or TV allowed.
  • No other person should enter the room during the exam.
  • Do not have books, papers, pens, calculators, or phones unless specifically allowed.
